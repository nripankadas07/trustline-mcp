# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be specific, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, protect private information, and accept maintainer decisions about scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
